Water in the Quran and Sunnah

Building a majel, maintaining a fountain or pointing out a spring is “giving water to drink”: the best charity according to the Prophet ﷺ. The texts are in Arabic, with the translation of their meaning.

من الحديث النبوي الشريف ﷺ
